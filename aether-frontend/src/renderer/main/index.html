AI Content Disclaimer

AetherArena uses artificial intelligence to generate content, including text, summaries, legal analysis, and research outputs. AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading.

Do not rely on AI outputs as professional, legal, medical, or financial advice. Always verify independently with qualified professionals.

Data Transparency

No data collection. AetherArena does not send any data to the developer or any third party. There is no telemetry, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
Runs locally. All processing, storage, and AI inference happen on your machine. Your documents, chat history, and personal data never leave your device unless you choose otherwise.
Cloud AI services (your choice). If you configure a cloud AI provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), your prompts and context are sent to that provider's servers under their terms of service. This is entirely optional and user-initiated.

Open-Source Credits

AetherArena is built on the shoulders of many open-source projects. We are grateful to the communities and individuals who make their work freely available.

Key components include Electron, FastAPI, Perplexica, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Three.js, Preact, and many more.
